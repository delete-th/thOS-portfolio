# Sound credits

## Sourced sounds

All three are from asset packs by **Kenney** (https://kenney.nl), released under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
CC0 doesn't require credit; it's given here anyway.

Each was trimmed of leading and trailing silence, set to the same loudness
(−20 dBFS RMS), and re-encoded as mono Ogg Vorbis with an MP3 fallback.

## Original sounds

Every `.wav` file here (`startup`, `click`, `window-open`, `window-close`, `minimize`,
`error`, `send`, `ambient`) is original to thOS, synthesized by
`scripts/generate-sounds.mjs`.
